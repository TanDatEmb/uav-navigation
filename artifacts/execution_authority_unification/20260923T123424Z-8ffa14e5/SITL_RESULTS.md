The one nominal `trajectory_rejected` is sample 347, generation 3, request 2, in session `135131-418913`. The adapter warning at wall `1790171531.305` says `rejected before acceptance validation: valid=0 command_present=1`; samples 346 and 348 were admitted, and no lease/Hold/identity/continuity failure followed. The recorded `/clock` and command header are within one 4 ms tick, but that does not prove the adapter's exact callback clock value. This isolated precheck rejection remains an observed transport boundary event, not silently reported as zero rejection.
