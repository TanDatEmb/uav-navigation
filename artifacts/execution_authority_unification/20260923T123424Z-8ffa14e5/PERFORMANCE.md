The same owner still keeps the bounded final publication callback under its mutex so revocation and publication linearize; planner solve, candidate validation, world sweep and polynomial evaluation remain outside.

Required focused fields: command publication p50/p95/p99/max, execution-owner lock wait, planning scheduling gap and adapter setpoint handoff gap. Compare the pinned repaired `long_featured`, seed 0, tracking-off sessions with matched cut sessions by session manifest and complete mission outcome. Do not infer a product deadline or performance benefit from a handful of runs.

Measurements and unavailable fields will be filled from the focused runtime sessions. `RUNTIME_UNVERIFIED` until then.
