# Performance measurement contract

The mechanical lock count is two execution-internal mutexes before and one after. That does not establish a latency gain. The same owner still keeps the bounded final publication callback under its mutex so revocation and publication linearize; planner solve, candidate validation, world sweep and polynomial evaluation remain outside.

Required focused fields: command publication p50/p95/p99/max, execution-owner lock wait, planning scheduling gap and adapter setpoint handoff gap. Compare the pinned repaired `long_featured`, seed 0, tracking-off sessions with matched cut sessions by session manifest and complete mission outcome. Do not infer a product deadline or performance benefit from a handful of runs.

The pre-cut runtime diagnostics contain `command_store_publish_us`, `command_transport_publish_us`, `command_transition_lock_wait_us` and `planning_scheduling_gap_us`. They do not isolate the two old execution-mutex waits. This cut adds only `execution_owner_publish_lock_wait_us`, sampled at the final owner mutex entry and exported as a diagnostic; it never authorizes execution. Thus owner lock wait has an after distribution but no directly comparable before distribution. The existing total publish and scheduling measurements remain comparable. Measurements and unavailable fields will be filled from the focused runtime sessions. `RUNTIME_UNVERIFIED` until then.
