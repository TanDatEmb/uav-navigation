# State Admission Test for every proposed persistent V2 field

Columns answer the required twelve questions in order: **1 independent information; 2 exactly derivable?; 3 why survive; 4 sole writer; 5 creation; 6 invalidation; 7 mutually exclusive?; 8 tagged union?; 9 authority/diagnostic; 10 replicated?; 11 loss if removed; 12 invariant/test.** `?` means UNRESOLVED; a field with any `?` is not admitted for implementation. These are *candidate records*, not proposed C++ layout.
