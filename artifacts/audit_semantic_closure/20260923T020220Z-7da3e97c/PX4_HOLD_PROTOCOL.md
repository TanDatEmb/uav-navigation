## Candidate transfer protocol

Keep orthogonal facts: request generation/target, command ACK/result, scheduled-operation active/completion, fresh `VehicleStatus` observation with source/receive time and executor identity, deactivation cause, retry deadline. A linear `NotRequested→Accepted→Completed→Confirmed` enum is rejected: Status may precede callback, callback may never come, and deactivation can occur at any stage.

**Candidate success:** a fresh, post-request PX4 `VehicleStatus` with `nav_state=AUTO_LOITER`, identity/time attributable to current request, plus confirmed loss/transfer of external-mode authority according to pinned firmware contract. The latter attribution is an external contract gap; `ModeCompleted` alone cannot grant authority. **Retry stop:** observed confirmed Hold or separately classified operator/failsafe/executor takeover; the latter is not Hold success. **ACK success + completion success + no status:** stay pending/retry (or enter explicit unconfirmed timeout/escalation state); never mark transfer complete. **Operator takeover:** explicit external takeover with pilot mode and timestamp, not requested Hold. **Failsafe:** explicit failsafe takeover, separately reported. **Lost status:** last cached AUTO_LOITER expires and ceases to confirm current authority. **Executor shutdown:** explicit terminal protocol observation; no callback silently grants a flight status.

- **Current behavior:** pending can clear on callback without status; cached status has no explicit freshness bound in Hold handler.
- **Desired target behavior:** separate request/ACK/completion/status/deactivation observations and bounded retry/error policy.
- **Information preserved:** order, request identity, status source/receive time, external cause, current executor charge.
- **Information derivable:** `confirmed` only from fresh approved status/authority witness; `retry_due` from request, deadline, status and takeover cause.
- **Behavior intentionally lost:** stopping retry on `ModeCompleted(Success)` or `Deactivated` alone; this is a policy change requiring approval.
- **New risk:** retry during PX4 transition could issue redundant commands; retry must use state and idempotency bounds from the actual firmware.
- **Required product tests:** all permutations in event model with mocked pinned library; firmware-specific status/executor trace for Loiter, operator takeover, failsafe and shutdown; duplicate/stale status and absent completion.

`SPECIFICATION_GAP`: project PX4 firmware revision/build, source-stamp and monotonicity guarantees for `VehicleStatus`, meaning of executor-charge transition relative to AUTO_LOITER, and safe retry timing. `RUNTIME_UNVERIFIED`: actual callback/VehicleStatus order and persistent Loiter completion.
