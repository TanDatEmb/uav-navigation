# Decisions and additional evidence

1. **PASS_THROUGH retention policy.** The diagnostic stream can preserve measured crossing and continuation as independent facts. Product owners must specify how long a crossing observation may survive and exactly which route/localization/odometry/reverse-progress events invalidate it before acceptance logic changes. A focused crossing-first/re-entry runtime episode is still missing; the failed-replan switch in F1/F2 did not arm.
2. **PX4 Hold success and retry policy.** Define a fresh `VehicleStatus` authority witness, the meaning of `ModeCompleted(Success/Deactivated)` for LOITER under the pinned `px4_ros2_interface_lib` and executed PX4 binary, and behavior for callback-before-status, operator takeover, failsafe and missing status. Six runs show ACK then AUTO_LOITER without a LOITER completion callback, but do not cover those other orderings.
3. **World/command lease policy after handover.** W2–W5 observed no resume after Hold; decide whether the intended invariant is an explicit upstream authority fence at downstream handover, and specify what recertification work may continue without command authority. The current 100 ms adapter lease and 500 ms world freshness were preserved. Representative timing distributions and adversarial interleavings are still required before any authority migration.
4. **E2 exported `onTrajectory()` API.** The native path does not currently call this exported recovery entry point, yet it remains installed/tested. Decide whether it is supported downstream compatibility or a legacy API before removing or reinterpreting recoverable braking. This policy decision does not block a strictly diagnostic shadow that can emit `Unknown`.

No requested decision is made by the instrumentation, its parser, or a shadow comparison report. No implementation or safety gate is changed on this branch to resolve these policies.
