# Terminal root cause and repair decision

**Causal classification of the gate failures:** `ACTUAL_TRACKING_ERROR` in the product's measured LIO execution frame during an EMERGENCY-derived STOPPED_HOLD. The measured state was 0.762/0.759 m from the exact endpoint, still moving at approximately 0.580/0.863 m/s; Core correctly rejected an endpoint beyond the 0.750 m execution-anchor contract. These are not false positives from stale odometry or endpoint identity. The category refers to the product's actual measured tracking state; it does not assert independent physical truth crossed 0.750 m.

**Causal chain observed:** old nominal command tracking exceeds its retained-command budget → one-shot measured-state emergency brake becomes active → its short analytic brake ends → adapter continues exact endpoint position hold → measured vehicle crosses the endpoint and oscillates away → Core's STOPPED_HOLD proximity gate fails → rejected command and eventual adapter Hold. Run 1 source explicitly logs old-command anchor 0.635 m vs 0.250 m tracking limit before emergency commit. The two gate rejections are reproduced numerically from raw odometry and exact command endpoints.

Run 1 misses a sustained measured-stationary planning window before the hold error grows. Run 2 briefly enters PlanFromRest at 65.220 s; the solve returns `kFailed`/`replan_code=-12`, and the vehicle accelerates again while the exact endpoint is retained. Thus the replacement was unavailable in both runs for different immediate reasons. Neither fact makes the 0.750 m gate incorrect.

**Unresolved deeper cause:** why the closed-loop PX4/vehicle follows an underdamped trajectory after switching to position hold, and whether the emergency stop envelope is achievable under approved dynamics. Those require controller/dynamics characterization and representative repeated evidence. Altering hold target, emergency planner profile, controller gains, or the 0.750 m gate from these traces would change flight behavior/policy without sufficient proof. The branch therefore applies no terminal behavior fix and cannot freeze a stable behavior-bearing SHA under the requested success gate.

The longest observed PX4 input recorder interval during the terminal hold was 71.062/57.800 ms on the steady clock. Neither reached the 100 ms receive lease, and the exact endpoint remained unchanged, but a contribution of these tails to the closed-loop response has not been isolated. No gap is silently treated as zero merely because simulation source time advanced less.

Evaluation-only truth displacement over each hold differs from LIO displacement by 0.058/0.044 m. Locally aligned physical endpoint-error estimates at rejection are 0.747/0.732 m. Both are near the gate, and the small measured overage cannot be dismissed as noise; they also prevent a claim that the physical body independently exceeded 0.750 m. Estimator-relative displacement and closed-loop stopping response must be separated before a behavior fix.

The independent truth speed rises from 0.161 to 0.600 m/s in run 1 and from 0.127 to 0.879 m/s in run 2 between analytic completion and rejection. This corroborates real post-completion motion, while the exact endpoint-distance threshold crossing remains specific to the authoritative LIO state.

**Decision:** retain the fail-closed response. Classify the two historical nominal failures as explained gate events, but the reliability blocker remains open. Next product work needs a bounded stopping/hold experiment with direct PX4 setpoint vs measured dynamics and an independently approved behavior change, followed by a new ≥10-run cohort.
