# State conservation decision

`STATE_CONSERVATION_MATRIX.csv` gives a disposition for all 239 behavioral candidates from the pinned prior scan, with semantic cluster, target owner, replacement, source declaration and confidence. Generated by `python3 -m tools.shadow_reducer.conservation <output.csv>` with an exact 239-row/key assertion. This is a **scoped candidate inventory**, not a count of every product object/member.

| Disposition | Count | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| KEEP | 158 | Preserve independent physical/temporal/protocol, worker or diagnostic information at its boundary. This is conservative, not a declaration of single-writer proof for every low-criticality field. |
| MOVE | 18 | Move semantic ownership, especially measured/accepted mission progress, active PX4 status witness and staged execution. |
| DERIVE | 14 | Derive projections such as `safety_suffix_active`, `command_available`, terminal/confirmation views and duplicate time units from canonical evidence. |
| MERGE | 22 | Fold mirrored lifecycle/identity into typed execution or Hold transfer representation while retaining distinct payload facts. |
| DELETE | 17 | Remove duplicated adapter planner recovery tuple, mirrored mission controller and redundant pending/readiness booleans after migration. |
| UNRESOLVED | 10 | Exact product writer/reader or external-owner semantics not proved; preserve until targeted migration review. |

The `UNRESOLVED` rows name the exact field and missing semantic owner; they are **not** a generic TODO. `DELETE`/`DERIVE` are target migration decisions, not edits made by this branch. Lexically clustered low-criticality fields marked `KEEP` remain explicit boundary information rather than being erased to inflate simplification. High-criticality pass crossing, committed stop, Hold protocol, world certificate and downstream fence are represented in executable model tests.
