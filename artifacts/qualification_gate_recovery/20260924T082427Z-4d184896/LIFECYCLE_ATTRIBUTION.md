This is a possible **evidence producer mapping gap**, not proof that the execution failed to activate. Conversely a request-only solve cannot be made a successful transaction.

Until exact direct-commit activation and retained-command bundle ownership can be reconstructed from producer-owned fields for every reference sample, `REFERENCE_LINEAGE_MISMATCH` remains valid. Do not assign a planner cycle by arrival order, sample proximity, or request identity alone. Unit/component evidence is needed before reducer change. Raw A1/A2/A3 remain NOT_EVALUABLE.

## Focused mapping repair

The scenario recorder now emits `activate/ACTIVATED` only when the runtime's own admission trace says `runtime_admission_succeeded=1` and `runtime_admission_disposition=IMMEDIATELY_COMMITTED`, with the same cycle, request, epoch and bundle generation. Staged and rejected candidates do not acquire this witness. This closes the direct-commit mapping gap for new captures; it does not retroactively repair missing export ownership or incomplete request-only transactions in A1/A2/A3. Three deterministic tests cover direct, staged and failed dispositions.
A fourth reducer integration test proves the exact direct-commit witness, authorization and attributed PX4 publish form one valid reference identity; it does not repair other missing phases by temporal matching.

In the ten-run new-source cohort the mapping repair operates, but lifecycle attribution still has 140 incomplete transactions. Event-set counts are 87 request-only, 22 request+export, 20 authorize+publish, 10 publish-only and one four-phase record lacking the complete reducer contract. These counts are from each report's `evaluation.lifecycle_reduction.unresolved`, with no inferred match. Thus the evaluator reason remains valid in all ten runs. Closing it requires producer-owned terminal outcomes for request-only failures and exact bundle/export lineage for independently observed authorize/publish samples. No nearest-time repair was applied.
